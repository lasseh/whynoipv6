# Curation: 2026-08-01 review of the Tranco top-1000 (research: apex/www content
# probe + ownership research per ambiguous host). Inclusion test: humans do not
# deliberately navigate to the apex; vanity aliases users actually type

# stay OUT. Tenant/app-hosting suffixes (github.io, sharepoint.com) are IN —
# humans only ever visit customer subdomains, never the listed apex.
#
