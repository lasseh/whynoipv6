Planned IPv4 outage

You are on IPv4.

This site is switched off over IPv4 today. It is running normally over IPv6. That is deliberate, and it is the entire point of this website.

Why No IPv6 keeps score of the websites that still have not turned on IPv6. On the 6th of every month we take our own advice and stop answering over IPv4 for the day. If you can read this, your connection is IPv4 only.

503 Service Unavailable Retry-Over-IPv6: ?1

What you can do

  • Ask your internet provider when they will give you IPv6. It is not an upgrade you pay for. It is the default on every modern network, and it has been a standard since 1998.
  • On a work or campus network, ask whoever runs it. The answer is usually that nobody has asked for it yet. Now someone has.
  • Try another connection. Mobile networks are often IPv6 by default, so your phone may well reach this site while your desk does not.

Come back tomorrow and the site will answer over IPv4 again.
